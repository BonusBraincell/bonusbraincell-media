**Title:** Why does airport security swab your hands?
**Description:**
Why does airport security swab your hands? The swab collects invisible residue, and a trace detector (usually an ion mobility spectrometer) identifies it by timing a molecule race.
Anyone who handles explosives can leave invisible traces on what they touch. The detector heats the swab so the residue turns to vapor, gives the molecules an electric charge (usually with a small radioactive source), and an electric field pulls them down a tube against a flow of gas. Each kind crosses at its own speed, in milliseconds; arrival time is checked against a library of explosives. Simplified model; peaks shown are illustrative.
