## YouTube Shorts (public, category 27 Education)
**Title:** Strait of Hormuz: why it moves your gas price
**Description:**
Why can one narrow strait move your gas price? Because crude oil trades in one global market: if oil can't get through the Strait of Hormuz, buyers bid for barrels elsewhere and prices tend to rise together, even for U.S. crude.
In 2025 only 8% of U.S. crude oil imports came from Middle East Gulf countries (EIA). But in 2024 about 20 million barrels a day moved through the strait, roughly a fifth of world oil use, and EIA estimated in 2025 that only about 2.6 million barrels a day of pipeline capacity could be available to bypass it (IEA's 2026 estimate: 3.5 to 5.5 million). Crude oil is about half of the retail price of a gallon of gasoline.
