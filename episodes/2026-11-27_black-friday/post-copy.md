**Title:** Why is it called Black Friday? Blame the police
**Description:**
Why is it called Black Friday? The name came from Philadelphia police by the early 1960s, not from stores getting "in the black."
On the day after Thanksgiving, suburban shoppers and tourists poured downtown (in some years ahead of the Army-Navy game), and police worked longer shifts through traffic jams, accidents and shoplifting. Merchants tried "Big Friday." It didn't stick, and the red-to-black profit story caught on in the late 1980s.
