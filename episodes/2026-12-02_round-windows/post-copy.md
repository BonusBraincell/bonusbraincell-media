Title: Why Are Airplane Windows Rounded? The Comet Clue
Description: Airplane windows are rounded because corners concentrate stress. After two de Havilland Comets broke apart in 1954, investigators pressurized a whole fuselage in a water tank until it split at a window corner, and the Elba wreckage showed fatigue at a roof window. The Comet's windows weren't sharp squares (a test report lists 3-inch corner radii), but stress there ran far higher than calculated. Later Comets got oval windows.
