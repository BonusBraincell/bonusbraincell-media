Title: Why Are Clocks Dropping the Leap Second?
Description: The world's timekeepers voted in October 2026 (CGPM Resolution 5) to make UTC continuous from May 20, 2027: no more leap seconds, with the gap to Earth's rotation allowed to reach one hour. 27 leap seconds were added from 1972 to 2016, nine of them in the 1970s; a faster-spinning Earth raised the odds of a first-ever negative leap second.
